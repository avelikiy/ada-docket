Why California's federal count reads low

California has its own access statute, the Unruh Civil Rights Act, which allows statutory damages that the ADA does not. A great deal of California access litigation is therefore filed in state court and never appears on a federal docket. This page counts federal filings only. Treat it as a floor for California, and a considerably lower one than for states without a damages statute of their own.
